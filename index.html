WHY CHEMISFREE?

Chemistry should be easier to access.

Chemistry and drug discovery rely on an enormous ecosystem of software, databases, workflows and scientific resources.

ChemIsFree brings these resources together in one accessible catalogue, making it easier to discover what already exists and easier for the community to share what they build.
