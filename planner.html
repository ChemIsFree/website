01

PROJECT CONTEXT

Do you have a specific target?

A protein, enzyme, receptor, nucleic-acid target, or other biological entity. We can search ChEMBL live and use the selected organism for UniProt and PDB lookups.

Suggestions update as you type. Choose a match to anchor activity retrieval to its ChEMBL target ID. If the target is absent, you can still enter its name and organism and continue.

LIVE CHEMBL BIOACTIVITY

Retrieve reported activity data

Only the selected endpoint is queried. Keep IC50, Ki, and EC50 separate: they describe different measurements and should not be pooled as if interchangeable. Activity values are reported measurements, not a uniformly curated potency series.

The export preserves assay IDs, units, relations, document IDs, and compound IDs; canonical SMILES are included when the ChEMBL molecule endpoint returns them. Inspect assay type, confidence, duplicates, and measurement conditions before modelling.
