THE PROCESS

From suggestion
to catalogue.

01

Submit

Fill in the structured GitHub form with the tool's basic information, area, tasks, access and official links.

02

Review

ChemIsFree administrators review the submission, verify the information and decide whether the resource belongs in the catalogue.

03

Approve

Accepted submissions are converted into structured catalogue data through the ChemIsFree GitHub workflow.

04

Publish

The approved resource becomes part of the public ChemIsFree catalogue.

WHAT BELONGS HERE

Useful beats
exhaustive.

ChemIsFree is intentionally curated. We are interested in resources that can genuinely help researchers working in chemistry, cheminformatics, molecular modelling, drug discovery and related scientific workflows.

This includes free and open-source applications, command-line tools, databases, libraries, datasets, workflows and educational resources.

+

Useful to chemistry or drug-discovery researchers

+

Freely accessible or open-source

+

Has an identifiable official source

+

Documented enough for someone to actually use
